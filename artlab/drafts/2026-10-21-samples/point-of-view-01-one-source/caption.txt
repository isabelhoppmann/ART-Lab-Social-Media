FORMAT

Point of View puts that opinion on one dark cover slide, then lets images do the rest. No disc, no slide counter, never more than one text slide.

CAPTION
ONE SOURCE
We don’t trust a room that is lit evenly. The rooms we remember have one source: a slot in a concrete ceiling, a round skylight, late sun across a sunken floor, a single globe left on at dusk. Light from one side gives a room a direction, somewhere to walk toward and somewhere to sit. Shadow isn’t what’s left over. It’s half the design.
