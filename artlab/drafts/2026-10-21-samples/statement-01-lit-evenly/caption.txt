CAPTION
ONE SOURCE
The rooms we remember have one source: a slot in a concrete ceiling, a round skylight, late sun across a sunken floor, a single globe left on at dusk. Light from one side gives a room a direction, somewhere to walk toward and somewhere to sit, and the shadow it leaves is as much a part of the design as the light.
