CAPTION
The colors in a firework come from metals. Strontium burns red, barium green, sodium yellow and copper blue. Blue is the hardest of the four to make.

Happy New Year.

Fireworks at the Winter Carnival in Steamboat Springs, Colorado. Photographs: Carol M. Highsmith, Library of Congress.
