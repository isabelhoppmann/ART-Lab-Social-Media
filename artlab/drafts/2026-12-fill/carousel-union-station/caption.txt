CAPTION
Daniel Burnham's Union Station opened in Washington in 1907, its main waiting room vaulted like the Baths of Diocletian in Rome. By 1981 the roof leaked so badly that a rainstorm brought pieces of the ceiling down, and most of the station closed. It reopened in 1988 after a restoration, and the vault, plain when it was built, came back gilded.

Union Station (1907), Daniel H. Burnham. Photographs: Carol M. Highsmith, Library of Congress.
