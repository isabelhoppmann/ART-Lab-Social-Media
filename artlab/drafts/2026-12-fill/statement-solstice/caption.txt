STATEMENT — "The shortest day deserves the best light." (Mon Dec 21, winter solstice; single slide)

CAPTION
Around 3200 BC, the builders of Newgrange, in Ireland's Boyne Valley, set a small opening called a roof-box above the tomb's entrance. At sunrise on the winter solstice, a thin beam passes through it, runs the length of the 19-meter passage and lights the chamber at the back for about 17 minutes.

Newgrange (c. 3200 BC), Brú na Bóinne, County Meath, Ireland.
