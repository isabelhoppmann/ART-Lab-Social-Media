CAPTION
In December the sun crosses the sky so low that, through a clear south window, it reaches all the way to the back of a room. Passive solar houses are built around that: a roof overhang sized so the high summer sun stays off the glass and the low winter sun comes straight in. A south window with nothing in front of it does much of that work on its own.
