CAPTION
In 1959, the Aluminum Specialty Company of Manitowoc, Wisconsin, began selling the Evergleam, a holiday tree with shiny aluminum branches. String lights on metal branches risked a short circuit, so the trees were lit from the floor by a rotating color wheel that turned them red, blue, green and gold. The company made more than a million. Sales faded after A Charlie Brown Christmas (1965) cast them as gaudy and fake.
