STATEMENT — "Every year ends with a light coming down." (Thu Dec 31, New Year's Eve; single slide)

CAPTION
The first ball came down from the roof of One Times Square on New Year's Eve 1907, to christen the New York Times' new headquarters. It was iron and wood, five feet across, weighed 700 pounds and was lit by 100 light bulbs. Today's ball is 12 feet across and weighs close to six tons.
