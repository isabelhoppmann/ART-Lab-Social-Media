FIELD NOTES 01 — CYSP 1 (6 slides + reel)

CAPTION
In 1956, the sculptor Nicolas Schöffer and a team of Philips engineers built CYSP 1: sixteen motorized plates on a rolling base, with sensors that registered light, color and sound. That summer the choreographer Maurice Béjart set his dancers beside it on the roof of Le Corbusier’s Cité Radieuse in Marseille. The dancers followed choreography, while the sculpture moved whenever the light and sound around it changed.

CYSP 1 (1956), Nicolas Schöffer. Choreography by Maurice Béjart. Music by Pierre Henry.
