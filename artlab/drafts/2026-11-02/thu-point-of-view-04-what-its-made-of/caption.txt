CAPTION
WHAT IT'S MADE OF
A wall should show what it's made of. Paint makes every room the same age, while stone, earth and lime plaster catch the light differently at every hour. The building we'd point to is Therme Vals in Switzerland, which Peter Zumthor built from some 60,000 slabs of quartzite quarried in the village, lit through narrow strips of glass between the roof slabs.
