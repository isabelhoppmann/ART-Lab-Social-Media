FRI NOV 6 — THE FUTURE, THEN 04 — Nakagin Capsule Tower (9 slides + reel)

CAPTION
In 1972, Kisho Kurokawa finished the Nakagin Capsule Tower in Tokyo: 140 factory-made rooms bolted onto two concrete cores, each with one round window. He meant the capsules to be replaced every 25 years or so, and none of them ever were. When the tower came down in 2022, 23 capsules were saved, and single capsules now belong to SFMOMA, MoMA, M+ in Hong Kong and the Museum of Modern Art, Wakayama.

Nakagin Capsule Tower (1970–1972), Kisho Kurokawa.
