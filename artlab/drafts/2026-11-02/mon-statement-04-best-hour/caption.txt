CAPTION
BEST HOUR
We think a great room has one hour it was built for. At the Salk Institute in La Jolla, which opened in 1965, Louis Kahn left the courtyard between the labs as bare travertine, on the advice of the architect Luis Barragán. A thin channel of water runs down the middle toward the Pacific, and around the spring and fall equinoxes, the sun sets in line with it.
