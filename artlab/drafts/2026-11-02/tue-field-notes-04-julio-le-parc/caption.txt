TUE NOV 3 — FIELD NOTES 04 — Julio Le Parc (9 slides + reel)

CAPTION
In 1966, Julio Le Parc won the Grand Prize for Painting at the Venice Biennale with mirrors, small motors and light. His group in Paris wanted people to take part in art, not stand back from it, and in 1972 he let a coin toss turn down a Paris retrospective. He died in May at 97, while preparing a Tate Modern show that runs until May 3, 2027.

Julio Le Parc (1928–2026). Julio Le Parc: Light. Colour. Action., Tate Modern.
