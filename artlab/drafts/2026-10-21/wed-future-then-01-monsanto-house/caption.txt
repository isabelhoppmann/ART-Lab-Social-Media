WED OCT 21 — THE FUTURE, THEN 01 — Monsanto House of the Future (9 slides + reel)

CAPTION
In 1957, Disneyland’s Tomorrowland opened the Monsanto House of the Future, a plastic home designed by two MIT architects for a chemical company that wanted to show what plastic could do. More than 20 million people walked through it. When Tomorrowland was redone in 1967, the wrecking ball bounced off and crews finally crushed the house with chains, and its foundation is still there, painted green and used as a planter.

Monsanto House of the Future (1957–1967), Marvin Goody and Richard Hamilton, MIT.
