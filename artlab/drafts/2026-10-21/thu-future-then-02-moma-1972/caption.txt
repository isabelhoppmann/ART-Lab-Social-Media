CAPTION
In 1972, the Museum of Modern Art asked a group of Italian designers to build the home of the future inside its galleries. They came back with a whole kitchen built into one white block, capsule rooms raised on legs, furniture shaped like pyramids and walls of cabinets that open into rooms, and each designer made a short film showing how their room would be lived in. The show ran from May to September 1972.

Italy: The New Domestic Landscape (1972), directed by Emilio Ambasz. Photographs: Leonardo LeGrand, The Museum of Modern Art Archives, New York.
