FRI OCT 23 — FIELD NOTES 02 — The Senster (7 slides + reel)

CAPTION
In 1970, Philips commissioned the sculptor Edward Ihnatowicz to build the Senster for the Evoluon in Eindhoven, a steel robot whose head turned toward gentle sounds and pulled away from loud ones. It came down in 1974 and was long thought destroyed, while its skeleton stood outdoors in Zeeland for four decades. In 2017, a university in Kraków bought it and restored it to working order.

The Senster (1970), Edward Ihnatowicz. Commissioned by Philips for the Evoluon.
