FRI OCT 23 — FIELD NOTES 02 — The Senster (7 slides + reel)

CAPTION
In 1970, Philips commissioned Edward Ihnatowicz to build the Senster for the Evoluon in Eindhoven: a hydraulic steel skeleton with four microphones and two Doppler radar units in its head. A computer read the room, and the head turned toward gentle sounds and drew back from loud ones, so the way to bring it closer was to lower your voice.

The Senster (1970), Edward Ihnatowicz. Commissioned by Philips for the Evoluon.
