STATEMENT 02 — "The future used to have rounded corners." (single text slide, 1080x1350)
Extra post for the week of Oct 19, not Field Notes.

CAPTION
ROUNDED CORNERS
The future we were promised in the 1960s and 70s came with soft edges: a plastic house curving out of a concrete core at Disneyland, capsule rooms with porthole windows, a television shaped like an astronaut's helmet. Somewhere along the way the future turned flat, square and glass-fronted, and we miss the version that looked like it was made to be touched.
