CAPTION
The future we were promised in the 1960s and 70s came with soft edges:

a chair made in one continuous curve,
a trolley of rounded drawers on wheels,
a chair of nesting tubes,
a red typewriter with a handle.

Somewhere along the way the future turned flat, square and glass-fronted, and we miss the version that looked like it was made to be lived in.

Panton Chair, Verner Panton; Boby and Tube Chair, Joe Colombo; Valentine, Ettore Sottsass and Perry King. Photographs: MoMA.
