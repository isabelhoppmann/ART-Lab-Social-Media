THU OCT 22 — WHY IT WORKS 01 — Arco (8 slides + reel)

CAPTION
In 1962, Achille and Pier Giacomo Castiglioni borrowed the logic of a street lamp for the dining room: a Carrara marble base in the corner and a steel arc that carries the light out over the table, with no fixture and no holes in the ceiling. A hole through the marble lets two people carry it on a broom handle, and every part of it, down to that hole, is in plain view.

Arco (1962), Achille and Pier Giacomo Castiglioni for Flos.
