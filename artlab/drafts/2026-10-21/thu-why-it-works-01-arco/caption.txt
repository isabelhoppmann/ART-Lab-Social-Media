THU OCT 22 — WHY IT WORKS 01 — Arco (5 slides + reel)

CAPTION
In 1962, Achille and Pier Giacomo Castiglioni borrowed the logic of a street lamp for the dining room: a Carrara marble base in the corner and a steel arc that carries the light out over the table, with no fixture and no holes in the ceiling. To move it, two people slide a broom handle through a hole in the marble and lift.

Arco (1962), Achille and Pier Giacomo Castiglioni for Flos.
