CAPTION
OBJECTS WITH FACES
We like machines that look back at you. The designer Marco Zanuso said his Algol television was a little dog looking up at its master. His TS 502 radio stays a plain box until it opens in half, his Black 201 hides its screen behind black plastic until it is switched on, and JVC's Videosphere put the picture inside an astronaut's helmet.

Algol (1964), TS 502 and Black 201, Marco Zanuso and Richard Sapper for Brionvega; Videosphere (1970), JVC. Photographs: The Museum of Modern Art, New York.
