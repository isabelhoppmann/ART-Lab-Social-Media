CAPTION
OBJECTS WITH FACES
We like machines that look back at you. Marco Zanuso said his Algol television was a little dog looking up at its master. His TS 502 radio stays a plain box until it opens, his Grillo phone folds in half, and the Black 201 hides its screen until it's switched on. Sony's 1959 set wears a visor and JVC's Videosphere a space helmet.

Algol, TS 502, Grillo and Black 201, Marco Zanuso and Richard Sapper; Videosphere, JVC; Portable television, Sony. Photographs: MoMA.
