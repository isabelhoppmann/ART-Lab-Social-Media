CAPTION
Richard Meier clad the Getty Center in Los Angeles in 16,000 tons of travertine from Bagni di Tivoli, near Rome, split along its natural grain by a guillotine built for the job. The rough faces show fossil leaves and feathers. Workers first set the fossil stones aside, and Meier had them put back into the walls to break up the pattern.

Getty Center (1997), Richard Meier. Photographs: Carol M. Highsmith, Library of Congress.
