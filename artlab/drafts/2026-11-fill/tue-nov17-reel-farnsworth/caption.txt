CAPTION
Mies van der Rohe finished the Farnsworth House in 1951 for Edith Farnsworth, a Chicago physician: a single glass room on steel columns, raised about five feet because the Fox River beside it floods. A wood-paneled core holds the kitchen, two bathrooms and a fireplace. Everything else is glass, so the walls change with the season outside.

Farnsworth House (1951), Ludwig Mies van der Rohe. Photographs: Carol M. Highsmith, Library of Congress.
