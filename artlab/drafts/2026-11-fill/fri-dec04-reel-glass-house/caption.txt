CAPTION
Philip Johnson finished his Glass House in New Canaan, Connecticut, in 1949, before Mies van der Rohe's Farnsworth House, the design he said gave him the idea. A brick cylinder holds the bathroom on one side and the fireplace on the other, and Johnson called the landscape outside his wallpaper. He added buildings across the 49 acres for decades, among them a pavilion in a pond, and lived there until 2005.

The Glass House (1949), Philip Johnson. Photographs: Carol M. Highsmith, Library of Congress.
