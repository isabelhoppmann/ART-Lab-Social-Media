CAPTION
Louis Kahn's Kimbell Art Museum in Fort Worth opened in 1972 under rows of concrete vaults shaped as cycloids. A narrow skylight runs along the top of each vault, and pierced aluminum reflectors hung beneath it throw the daylight back up onto the curved concrete, which spreads it through the galleries. At the entrance, Kahn left the vaults open as porches beside pools of water.

Kimbell Art Museum (1972), Louis I. Kahn. Photographs: Carol M. Highsmith, Library of Congress.
