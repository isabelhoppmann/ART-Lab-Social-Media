CAPTION
Fresh snow reflects 80 to 90 percent of the sunlight that reaches it, so a window onto a snowfield lights a room from below as well as from above. The light reaches ceilings and the undersides of stairs, which daylight usually leaves in shade. In these four rooms, winter light comes in low and stays soft from morning until the moon is up.
