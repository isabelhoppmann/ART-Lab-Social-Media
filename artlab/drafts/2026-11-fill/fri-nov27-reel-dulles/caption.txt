CAPTION
Eero Saarinen wanted Dulles to sit between earth and sky: a concrete roof hung on cables between two rows of outward-leaning piers. He died in 1961, and his office finished the terminal for its dedication in November 1962. Passengers never walked out to their planes; mobile lounges built by Chrysler and the Budd Company drove them across the field.

Washington Dulles International Airport, main terminal (1962), Eero Saarinen. Photographs: Carol M. Highsmith, Library of Congress.
