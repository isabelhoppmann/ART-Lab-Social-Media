CAPTION
Colored glass works by absorbing part of the light that hits it. A blue pane holds back most of the long, warm wavelengths and passes the short ones, so the patch it throws on a white wall is the color of the glass. Paint does the same in reverse, reflecting its own color and absorbing the rest. Each of these four rooms takes its color from one window or one wall.
