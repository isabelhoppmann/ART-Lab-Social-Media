CAPTION
When the Statue of Liberty was finished in 1886, its copper skin was brown, like a new penny. Salt air turned it green over the next 25 to 30 years. That green layer, the patina, protects the copper underneath, which is only about 3/32 of an inch thick, roughly two pennies stacked.
