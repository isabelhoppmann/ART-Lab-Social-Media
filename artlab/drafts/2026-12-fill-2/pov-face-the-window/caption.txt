CAPTION
In the 1660s, Venice held a monopoly on fine mirrors. Louis XIV's minister Colbert wanted France to make its own, so in 1665 he founded a royal mirror works and enticed Venetian glassworkers to Paris to staff it. Its first big order was the Hall of Mirrors at Versailles: 357 mirrors set in 17 arches, directly across from 17 windows onto the gardens.
