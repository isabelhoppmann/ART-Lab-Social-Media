CAPTION
In the Hall of Mirrors at Versailles, 17 tall windows look onto the gardens. On the wall facing them, 17 arches are filled with more than 350 mirrors, which throw the window light back across the 73-meter gallery. Jules Hardouin-Mansart built it between 1678 and 1684.

Images: ART Lab.
