STATEMENT — "The darkest months deserve the warmest rooms." (January; single slide)

CAPTION
In midwinter, Copenhagen gets about seven hours of daylight, and the sun sets before four in the afternoon. Denmark also burns more candles per person than any other country in Europe, close to three times the EU average, according to the European Candle Association. When the day is that short, the light inside a room has to do the rest of the work.

ALT TEXT
Cream capitals on a deep green background, in alternating serif and wide sans lines, read: The darkest months deserve the warmest rooms.

HYPOTHESIS
Prediction: more saves than the December statements, because January is when people are home and rethinking rooms, and the candle figure is a fact people quote. If it trails, statements need a holiday hook to travel. Share trigger: a design opinion + a fact worth repeating.
