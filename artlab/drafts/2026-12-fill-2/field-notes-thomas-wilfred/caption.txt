FIELD NOTES — Thomas Wilfred and lumia (carousel: 9 slides 1080x1350 + reel 1080x1920, 40.5s, silent)

CAPTION
In 1922, Thomas Wilfred played a concert of light in New York on the Clavilux, an instrument he built in 1919 to play moving color from a keyboard. He called the art lumia. The Museum of Modern Art commissioned his Lumia Suite, Opus 158 in 1963, and it played in its own dark room there until 1980, long after his death in 1968. Yale restored it in 2017.

Lumia Suite, Opus 158 (1963–64), Thomas Wilfred. The Museum of Modern Art.
