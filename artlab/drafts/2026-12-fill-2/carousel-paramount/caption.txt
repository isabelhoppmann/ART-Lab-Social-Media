CAPTION
The Paramount opened in Oakland on December 16, 1931: an Art Deco movie palace by Timothy Pflueger, with 3,476 seats. It closed as a cinema on September 15, 1970. Two years later the Oakland Symphony bought it as its new home, restored it, and reopened it on September 22, 1973.

Paramount Theatre (1931), Timothy L. Pflueger, Miller & Pflueger. Photographs: Carol M. Highsmith, Library of Congress.
