CAPTION
In 1946 Edgar Kaufmann, the client behind Fallingwater, had Richard Neutra build him a house in Palm Springs for the winter months, away from Pittsburgh. A year later Frank Sinatra gave E. Stewart Williams his first house commission. It was finished in time for a New Year's party, and named for the two palms beside the pool.

Kaufmann House (1946), Richard Neutra; Twin Palms (1947), E. Stewart Williams; Del Marcos Hotel; Palm Springs City Hall; an Alexander Construction Co. house. Photographs: Carol M. Highsmith, Library of Congress.
