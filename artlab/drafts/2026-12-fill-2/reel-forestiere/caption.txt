CAPTION
Fresno summers run past 100 degrees, and the ground was hardpan too hard to farm. So Baldassare Forestiere, a Sicilian immigrant, dug down. From 1906 until he died in 1946 he carved rooms, passages and courtyards by hand across about 10 acres, and cut skylights so citrus trees could grow underground, up through the openings toward the sun.

Forestiere Underground Gardens (1906–1946), Baldassare Forestiere. Photographs: Carol M. Highsmith, Library of Congress.
