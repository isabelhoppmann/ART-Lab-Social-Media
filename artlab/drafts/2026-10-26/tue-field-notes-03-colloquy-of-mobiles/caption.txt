TUE OCT 27 — FIELD NOTES 03 — Colloquy of Mobiles (9 slides + reel)

CAPTION
In 1968, at Cybernetic Serendipity in London, Gordon Pask hung five mobiles that courted each other with light. Two males sent out flashing beams, three females learned to aim mirrors to send the light back, and visitors could walk in and interrupt. Fifty years later, a design school in Detroit rebuilt all five, and the work now belongs to ZKM in Karlsruhe.

Colloquy of Mobiles (1968), Gordon Pask, ICA London. Reconstruction (2018), Paul Pangaro and TJ McLeish.
