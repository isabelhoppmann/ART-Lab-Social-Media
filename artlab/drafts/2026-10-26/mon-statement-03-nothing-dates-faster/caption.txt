CAPTION
OLD FUTURES
We think nothing dates faster than the future. At the 1964 New York World's Fair, visitors sat in a booth and saw the face of the person they were calling, sometimes at Disneyland. Nine years later, the service had fewer than 500 customers. We still collect old futures like that one, because they show what people hoped for before anyone knew how it would turn out.
