CAPTION
LOW LIGHT
Most rooms keep their light too high. After dark, light near the floor, at the height of a step or a seat, pulls a room in close and lets the ceiling go dark. Our favorite proof is the conversation pit Alexander Girard designed for the Miller House in 1957, a square of seating five steps down, where the whole evening happened below eye level.
