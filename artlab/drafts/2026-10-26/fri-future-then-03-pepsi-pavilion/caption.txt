FRI OCT 30 — THE FUTURE, THEN 03 — Pepsi Pavilion, Expo '70 (8 slides + reel)

CAPTION
At Expo '70 in Osaka, Pepsi handed its pavilion to a group of American and Japanese artists and engineers. The artist Fujiko Nakaya wrapped the dome in fog, and inside, a 90-foot Mylar mirror hung visitors upside down over their own heads. Not long after the fair opened, Pepsi ended the artists' program. Nakaya kept working with fog, and her fog sculpture has run beside the Guggenheim Bilbao since 1998.

Pepsi Pavilion, Expo '70 (1970), Experiments in Art and Technology (E.A.T.); fog sculpture, Fujiko Nakaya.
