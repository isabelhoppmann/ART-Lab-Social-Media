CAPTION
Frank Lloyd Wright finished the Johnson Wax Administration Building in Racine, Wisconsin, in 1939. Its Great Workroom stands on columns nine inches across at the base that open into lily pads about 18 feet wide. Wisconsin's inspectors doubted they would hold, so a test column was loaded until it carried 60 tons, five times the requirement. Daylight comes in through Pyrex glass tubing instead of windows.

S.C. Johnson Administration Building (1936–1939), Frank Lloyd Wright. Photographs: Carol M. Highsmith, Library of Congress.
