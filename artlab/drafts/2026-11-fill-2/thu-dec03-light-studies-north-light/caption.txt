CAPTION
Painters have long asked for a studio facing north. In the northern hemisphere, a north window doesn't take direct sun, so its light comes from the open sky, cool and soft, at nearly the same angle and color all day. A color mixed in the morning still looks right by late afternoon. These five rooms are built around that one steady source.
