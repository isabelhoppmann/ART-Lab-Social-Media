CAPTION
The Library of Congress began inside the Capitol. When it ran out of room, it moved across the street into the Thomas Jefferson Building, which opened in 1897. Members of Congress didn't have to walk over: they sent requests by pneumatic tube, and books came back through a tunnel under First Street on an electric chain conveyor.

Thomas Jefferson Building, Library of Congress (1897). Photographs: Carol M. Highsmith, Library of Congress.
