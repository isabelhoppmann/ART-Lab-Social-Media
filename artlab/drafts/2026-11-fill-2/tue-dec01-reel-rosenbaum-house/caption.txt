CAPTION
In 1940, Stanley and Mildred Rosenbaum, newly married, moved into the house Frank Lloyd Wright designed for them in Florence, Alabama: one story of cypress, brick and glass under cantilevered roofs. It is his only house in the state. When a fourth child arrived, Wright added a wing in 1948. The Rosenbaums lived there until 1999, and the city opened it as a museum in 2002.

Rosenbaum House (1940), Frank Lloyd Wright. Photographs: Carol M. Highsmith, Library of Congress.
