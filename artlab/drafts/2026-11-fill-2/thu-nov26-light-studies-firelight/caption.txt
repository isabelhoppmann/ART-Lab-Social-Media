CAPTION
A candle flame measures around 1,850 kelvin on the color temperature scale, against roughly 5,500 for daylight, and the lower the number, the warmer the light. Fire also sits low in a room, so it lights the table and the people around it and leaves the ceiling in shadow. Four rooms lit only by fire, for the evenings when everyone stays late.
