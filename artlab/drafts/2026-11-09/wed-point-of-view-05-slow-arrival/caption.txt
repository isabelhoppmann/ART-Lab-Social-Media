CAPTION
SLOW ARRIVAL
We like a building that makes you wait for the big room. Frank Lloyd Wright did it at the Guggenheim in New York, which opened in 1959: you come in under a low, hovering ceiling, and then the rotunda opens above you up to the skylight. Our four rooms follow the same order, from a dark passage to a column of daylight. We would rather walk toward a room than see all of it from the door.

Images: ART Lab.
