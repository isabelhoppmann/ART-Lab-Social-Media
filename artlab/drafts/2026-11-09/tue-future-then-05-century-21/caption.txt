CAPTION
Seattle's 1962 World's Fair, the Century 21 Exposition, built its Space Needle in less than a year. Edward Carlson, chair of the fair's commission, had sketched a restaurant on a tower in 1959. The paint colors had names: Astronaut White legs, Orbital Olive core, Re-entry Red halo, Galaxy Gold top. In 2018, the level that once held its revolving restaurant got a revolving glass floor.

Space Needle (1962), John Graham & Company; Pacific Science Center (1962), Minoru Yamasaki. Photographs: Carol M. Highsmith, Library of Congress.
