MON NOV 9 — STATEMENT 05: "The best rooms change while you sit in them." (single slide, 1080x1350, ink ground)

CAPTION
SIT STILL
The best rooms change while you sit in them. We think of James Turrell's Twilight Epiphany at Rice University in Houston, open since 2012: a 72-foot square roof with a 14-foot square opening to the sky. At sunrise and sunset, colored light moves across the underside of the roof while the sky in the opening brightens or darkens. You stay in one seat, and the whole room changes around you.
