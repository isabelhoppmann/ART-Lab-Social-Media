=== RESEARCH ===
- Researchers posted DECOWAM to arXiv this week, introducing a decoupled whole-body world-action model for legged mobile manipulation that separates locomotion and arm control into dedicated model heads while sharing a unified world representation, enabling quadruped robots with arms to perform dexterous manipulation during locomotion. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20114
