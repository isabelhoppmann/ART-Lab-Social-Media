=== RESEARCH ===
- Weiwei Gu et al. introduced RoboReel in "Monkey See, Can Monkey Do? A Benchmark for Evaluating Robot Skill Learning by Observation" (arXiv:2609.08209, submitted September 10), pairing 3,000 real human demonstration videos with 2,000 simulated robot trajectories across ten tabletop manipulation tasks to create a unified benchmark for evaluating policies that learn by watching people. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08209
- A 31-page preprint "No Free Checker: A Survey of Verifiers for Robot Policies" (arXiv:2609.09250, submitted September 8) surveys roughly 150 verifiers—from reward models and success detectors to temporal-logic monitors—and concludes that credibility inevitably falls as availability rises, so no single verification approach provides both qualities at once. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09250
