=== ROBOTICS & AI ===
- Anthropic's IPO priced at $83 per share on November 18 (above its $70-78 range), raising approximately $81 billion; shares began trading today on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT at an implied fully diluted valuation above $1.4 trillion. https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering

- ScaleVLA (arXiv:2611.21607, November 16, 2026) scales vision-language-action models to 552 billion parameters with a trainable action diffusion transformer, achieving 216 ms median latency per action chunk. https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21607
