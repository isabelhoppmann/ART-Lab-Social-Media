- Researchers introduced VLA-ULAP (arXiv:2609.18663, submitted September 16), a system that interleaves cloud-based VLA calls with a 7.4M-parameter edge predictor, cutting 49–77% of server roundtrips while retaining 95–97.5% of success rate and reducing per-inference energy from 50.5 J (cloud GR00T on RTX A6000) to 0.18 J on a Jetson Orin Nano. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18663
