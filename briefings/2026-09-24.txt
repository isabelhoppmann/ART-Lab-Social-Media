=== RESEARCH ===
- "Think Like a World Model, Act Like a VLA" (arXiv:2609.24682, submitted September 21) adds a feature-alignment loss to standard VLA training using a frozen world model's cached representations, giving the deployed policy implicit world-state awareness without running the world model at inference — the resulting policy runs at 32 ms and 1.86 GB on a consumer device and substantially outperforms the undistilled baseline on manipulation benchmarks. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24682
- DexTacWAM (arXiv:2609.24976, submitted September 21) fuses visual and tactile signals into a world-action model for dexterous manipulation, reaching a 70.6% average success rate on six contact-rich tasks on a 22-DoF bimanual platform versus 38.0% for the strongest baseline. https://dextacwam.github.io/
