- The FCC announced a ban on imports of all new foreign-made humanoid and quadruped robots, citing cybersecurity and national security risks in a move that primarily targets Chinese manufacturers, which hold roughly 85% of the global humanoid robot market; the rule applies only to newly authorized models and does not affect units already purchased. https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/us-bans-foreign-made-humanoid-robots-targeting-china-national-security-rcna589777
