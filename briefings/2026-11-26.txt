=== ROBOTICS & AI ===
- NVIDIA told its server manufacturing partners on November 26 to halt AI server shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, and Indonesia, responding to a US Commerce Department rule that took effect November 19 restricting overseas data centers from providing substantial AI computing to China-linked customers. https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/11/63661506/nvidia-pauses-server-shipments-to-4-southeast-asian-nations-as-us-tightens-china-ai-chip-controls-report
- Google's autonomous Cogentic AI research system (built on Gemini 4) published six original mathematics papers on November 25, including the first unconditional subexponential integer factoring algorithm in roughly 30 years; cryptographer Scott Aaronson notes the result has no immediate RSA cryptographic risk. https://www.rdworldonline.com/google-cogentic-ai-integer-factoring-bounds-lean-proofs/

=== RESEARCH ===
- Embodied-Harness (arXiv:2611.27689, submitted November 20, revised November 23) proposes a dual-agent harness that improves robotic manipulation through recursive self-improvement of its execution framework — without updating model weights — reporting a 96.7% average success rate across three real-world tasks. https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27689
