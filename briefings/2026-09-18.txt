=== ROBOTICS & AI ===
- Japan's ugo Inc. unveiled ugo Nova on September 16, 2026, a wheeled dual-arm semi-humanoid robot built to collect physical-AI training data through teleoperation, developed as part of a NEDO-commissioned national project for a domestically produced general-purpose robot, with mass production targeted for 2027. https://www.euronews.com/video/2026/09/16/from-nail-hammering-to-drilling-japans-new-robots-push-physical-ai-forward

=== RESEARCH ===
- TEMPO (arXiv 2609.16864, accepted at CoRL 2026, submitted September 15) shows that augmenting a pretrained VLA with a frozen video model's motion summary plus a proprioceptive-history token resolves both motion ambiguity and state aliasing, substantially improving success rates on dynamic manipulation tasks where single-frame policies fail. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16864
