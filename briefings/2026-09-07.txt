=== RESEARCH ===
- Researchers from PrimeBot Research Institute, Peking University, and Crobotia released arXiv:2609.03591 on September 3, presenting XR-2, a vision-language-action model trained on 1,500 hours of diverse bimanual household manipulation demonstrations; the study finds task success rates scale consistently with both expert demo volume and DAgger on-policy correction data, offering a concrete scaling roadmap for household manipulation. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03591
